[Agentic AI Test Checklist](https://huggingface.co/datasets/srinivaspadmanabhuni/agentic-ai-test-checklist-110) is an open checklist of 110 tests across 4 pillars for checking what AI agents actually do, with worked examples, labelled traces and responsible-AI additions. The open-source [agentest](https://github.com/srpadmanabhuni/agentest) runner checks agent traces against it and reports coverage per test.
