- **pytest:** add a pytest plugin inside `agentest-ai` (entry point `pytest11`, no new dependency; `-p no:agentest` turns it off). `@pytest.mark.agentest(trace=..., checks=["T1-018", "T4-003"])` or the `agentest_trace` and `agentest_record` fixtures assert that a trace passes the checks you name, and `agentest.assert_checks(trace, ids)` does the same without pytest. A failure lists each check that did not pass with what it asserts and the evidence it found, and `n/a` counts as not passed unless `allow_na=True`. Defaults come from the `agentest_trace`, `agentest_card`, `agentest_tools` and `agentest_mode` ini options. Adds `docs/integrations/pytest.md` and `examples/pytest_plugin/`. Works with pytest 7.0 and newer. Verdicts and the published count of 165 tests are unchanged.
