# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in agentest to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you were wrong.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, Discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.
Report problems privately to the maintainer at **srinivas AT testAIng.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may warn, suspend or ban participants
who break this code, following the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines.
